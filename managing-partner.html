JOYCE NDEHI

Legal counsel with an eye for resolution.

Joyce Ndehi is an Advocate of the High Court of Kenya and leads Joyce Ndehi & Company Advocates in Kiambu Town.

Her public professional profile identifies training and experience across civil and commercial law, probate and administration, conveyancing, legal writing, criminal law and trial advocacy.

Her listed advisory areas also include tax and copyright, while the firm's client materials place emphasis on mediation, succession and practical legal support.
