WHO WE ARE

A practice built around understanding.

Joyce Ndehi & Company Advocates is based at Telcom Exchange House on Biashara Street, Kiambu Town. We support individuals, families and businesses across personal, property and commercial legal matters.

Our services combine advisory, transactional and dispute-resolution work, including mediation, succession, conveyancing, commercial law, data protection, intellectual property and litigation.

We place particular emphasis on helping parties approach conflicts constructively where mediation or negotiation may be suitable, while remaining available for more formal legal processes when required.
