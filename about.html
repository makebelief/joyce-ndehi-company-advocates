About the firm

Practical legal support from Kiambu Town.

Joyce Ndehi & Company Advocates is based at Telcom Exchange House on Biashara Street, Kiambu Town, serving individuals, families and businesses across a range of personal, property and commercial legal matters.

Counsel built around the problem that needs solving.

The practice combines advisory, transactional and dispute-resolution work. Public professional information and the firm's own client materials identify mediation, succession, property and conveyancing, commercial law, data protection, intellectual property and related legal advisory work among its areas of focus.

The firm also communicates a strong commitment to mediation: helping parties work through disagreements confidentially and constructively where that approach is appropriate.
