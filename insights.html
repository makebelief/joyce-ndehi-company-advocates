When mediation may be worth considering

Mediation offers parties a confidential process for discussing a dispute with the assistance of a neutral mediator. It can be relevant in family, succession, property, workplace, commercial and other civil disputes where the parties are able to explore a negotiated outcome.

Succession planning and estate administration

Wills, probate or letters of administration, identifying estate assets and beneficiaries, and the eventual distribution of an estate involve distinct legal steps. Early advice can help families understand the process before disagreements arise.

Property transactions begin with verification

Conveyancing involves more than signing an agreement. Searches, ownership information, transaction documents, consents and completion steps should be reviewed carefully before parties commit funds or transfer interests.

Data protection is a governance issue

Organisations that collect or use personal information should understand the legal basis for processing, the information they hold and the safeguards expected when managing it.
